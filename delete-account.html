Delete Your Account

Last updated: November 2026

You can delete your KS Kirana account and its personal data at any time, without contacting us.

1. Delete it in the app (instant)

  • Open the KS Kirana app and sign in.
  • Go to the Profile tab.
  • Tap Delete My Account.
  • Enter your password and tap Delete Account.

Your account is deleted immediately. The same steps work on the KS Kirana website after signing in.

2. Or ask us to delete it

Email koteswararao7489@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my KS Kirana account". We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

3. What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password).
  • Your profile: name, phone number, language, and notification settings.
  • Your saved delivery addresses.

4. What is kept

  • Past orders and payment references, kept as required for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention. They are no longer linked to a login.

See our Privacy Policy for details on how we handle data.
